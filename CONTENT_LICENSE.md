## What CC BY-SA 4.0 means for you

You are free to **share** (copy and redistribute) and **adapt** (remix,
transform, build upon) the content for any purpose, even commercially, as long
as you:

- **Give credit** — mention "Fashion in Cambodia contributors" and link to this

<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode>.

## When you contribute content

By submitting content (through a pull request, the Keystatic editor, a GitHub
issue or the website's suggestion form) you confirm that:

1. You wrote it yourself, or you have the right to share it.
2. Photos are your own, or you have the photographer's permission to release them
   under CC BY-SA 4.0. Do **not** upload photos copied from Facebook, Google Maps
   or shop websites without permission.
3. You agree that it is published under CC BY-SA 4.0.
