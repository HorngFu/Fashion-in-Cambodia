## Can I buy things on this site?

No. Fashion in Cambodia is a directory, not a shop. It tells you which shops sell what and how to contact them. You buy directly from the shop.

## Is it free?

Yes, for everyone. Shops are listed for free, and no shop can pay for a better place in the list.

## Who runs it?

A community of volunteers. The code and the content are open source, so anyone can help or reuse them.

## How do you check the shop information?

Volunteers check shops by visiting, calling or confirming with the owner. Checked shops show a **Verified** badge and the date of the last check.

## My shop is listed with wrong information, or I want it removed

Use the **Report wrong information** link on your shop's page and tell us what to change.
