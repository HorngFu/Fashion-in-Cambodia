## What we collect

- **No accounts and no cookies.** You can use the whole site without signing in.
- **No advertising or tracking.** We do not sell or share data.
- **Hosting logs.** The site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and the pages requested) to keep the service working and secure.

## When you suggest a shop or report a mistake

Suggestions and reports are made on GitHub and are **public**. Do not include private information such as personal phone numbers or home addresses.

## Shop information

The directory lists public business information only: the shop's name, location, business contacts and what it sells. Shop owners can ask us to correct or remove a listing using the **Report wrong information** link on the shop's page.
